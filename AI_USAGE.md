# AI Usage Summary

Throughout this assignment, I primarily used ChatGPT and Codex as part of a structured, incremental development process.

At the beginning of the project, I used ChatGPT to understand the requirements and break the project down into small, clearly defined parts. After creating the development roadmap, ChatGPT helped me write a detailed and focused prompt for Codex at each stage. Only then did I move on to implementing that specific part.

I preferred this approach over giving the entire project to an AI tool at once because working step by step makes it much easier to understand what changed at each point. If a bug appears, it is relatively easy to identify the stage in which it was introduced and the relevant area of the code to inspect. This is more manageable than dealing with many simultaneous changes and trying to determine which one caused the problem.

The prompts created with ChatGPT were highly detailed. They specified not only what needed to be implemented, but also what should not be changed, the expected behavior, the edge cases that should be considered, and how to verify that the stage worked correctly. This helped keep Codex focused on the specific task without expanding into unrelated parts of the system.

As a result, I usually did not need many rounds of corrections or repeated prompts. In most stages, the initial prompt was precise enough for the implementation to be very close to what I had planned on the first attempt.

I also used ChatGPT to review technical topics that I needed to understand before implementation, including React, FastAPI, JWT, database interactions, external APIs, daily content, feedback, and edge cases. It was important to me to first understand what we were building, how the mechanism should work, and why a particular solution was chosen. Only after that did I ask Codex to make the corresponding change in the code.

I followed the same method when designing the UI. I first found designs and visual styles that I liked and shared screenshots with ChatGPT. We then refined which elements I wanted to draw from them, and ChatGPT translated that direction into a detailed design prompt for Codex. This meant that I did not use a broad instruction such as "make it look good." Instead, I established a clear and specific visual direction in advance.

Overall, I used ChatGPT mainly for planning, breaking down the assignment, learning, and creating precise prompts, while Codex was responsible for implementing each stage in the code. This workflow was comfortable and effective for me because it kept the project organized, made bugs easier to locate, reduced repeated corrections, and helped me maintain control and understanding throughout every stage of the process.
